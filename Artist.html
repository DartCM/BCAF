Raine

Raine, who uses the Instagram handle raine.5149, is a young and gifted queer artist. They are passionate about sharing their stories and characters online. Since this is their first time at an event like this, they are thrilled to showcase and sell copies of their existing artwork. They also specialise in dreamlike landscapes and scenery.

lilredbeany

Alongside their soft, cosy illustration style, lilredbeany is also the creator of a charming Webtoon series that blends heartfelt storytelling with expressive character art. Their comic brings everyday emotions to life through warm palettes and gentle humour, making it a favourite among readers who love character‑driven narratives.

At the Festival, lilredbeany will be showcasing artwork from their Webtoon, offering prints, stickers, and possibly even live sketches of fan‑favourite characters!

Instagram: @lilredbeany

freakyfreaq

Freakyfreaq is an experimental artist whose dramatic style extends into their Webtoon series, where eerie atmospheres, bold contrasts, and surreal storytelling take centre stage. Their comic explores strange worlds and expressive characters, blending dark fantasy with emotional depth in a way that keeps readers hooked.

At this year's Festival, fans can expect exclusive Webtoon‑themed prints, concept art, and a look into the creative process behind their most striking episodes.

Instagram: @freakyfreaq
